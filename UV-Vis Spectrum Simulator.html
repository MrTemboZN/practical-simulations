Concept map
  • Conjugation: Extending π‑conjugation usually red‑shifts (bathochromic) λmax and increases intensity (hyperchromic).
  • Transitions:
    • π→π*: Allowed, generally strong (K/E/B bands).
    • n→π*: Forbidden/weak, small ε (R band), often at longer λ in carbonyls.
  • Chromophores: Light‑absorbing units (e.g., C=C, C=O, aromatic ring, –NO₂, –N=N–).
  • Auxochromes: Groups like –OH/–OR/–NH₂/–NR₂/–X that modify λmax and ε via resonance/inductive effects.
  • K/B/E/R bands:
    • K: π→π* in conjugated systems (incl. polyenes & enones).
    • B & E: Aromatic (benzenoid) bands; E typically higher energy (shorter λ) and stronger than B.
    • R: n→π* in carbonyl‑type systems; weak intensity.
